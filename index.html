Custom Life Songs

About Custom Life Songs

I am passionate about capturing the essence of an individual through music! I love talking to people about their lives and loved ones. Through these conversations, I learn what makes each person unique and special, and synthesize what people say into personalized songs.

I allow the people to choose the mood of music that resonates with them, whether it's joyful, reflective, nostalgic, etc. My mission is to create a lasting musical tribute that honors a life journey, preserving their memories in a beautiful and meaningful way.

Whether you are a family wanting to commemorate your parent's life, or someone wanting to leave a legacy song for your children, I'd love to help. Let's create a soundtrack to your story together!

Hello

Welcome to my world of custom songwriting! I am a passionate songwriter and mental health professional based in the Pacific Northwest, with over 30 years of experience playing guitar and writing songs, including 15 years dedicated to custom songwriting. My approach is both heartfelt and engaging, as I strive to learn about the unique stories of each individual I work with. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, or a tribute to someone's life, my songs capture the essence of personal experiences and emotions.

I specialize in creating songs that help people tell their life stories, making them a meaningful gift, especially for older adults. The process involves intimate interviews, allowing me to connect with the person for whom the song is being written, as well as their loved ones. I believe that everyone has a story worth telling, and I am here to help honor those stories through music.

As an active member of the songwriting community in the region, I am connected to many talented musicians, which enriches my creative process. Let's work together to create a beautiful legacy project that resonates with you and your loved ones.

Legacy Projects

A legacy project is orchestrated by the individual the song is for, and/or any loved ones who would like to be involved. This can look a variety of ways, including live interviews, and/or having loved ones fill out questionnaires, depending on the number of people involved. You may involve as many people's input as you wish.

The interviews and questionnaires are targeted at finding out the most important details of the person's life. I will synthesize all of the answers, pulling out the most important themes and memories in order to create a fully unique song, tailored to tell the story of a life. These are great group gifts! I am happy to come and perform your song, and you can even choose to have it professionally recorded and produced!

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the interviews entail? ▼

Individual interviews typically last for approximately an hour or two, where I will ask questions about your life, trying to pull out what has been most special to you and/or your loved ones. I will also play various types of music for you, so that you can pick the style of song that you would like. I may also require a written questionnaire.

For group processes, the process varies, but I will usually do a combination of interviews and questionnaires, depending on the number of people involved. Group songs require a point person, who will contact participants with the form, and who I may have follow-up questions for.

For other types of songs, interviews and forms depend on the song type and process (individual or group).

Can I add a live performance? ▼

Yes! Live performances are available for an extra cost, depending on availability. I would love to come and perform your song for you and/or your loved ones. Just let me know at the time of your booking when and where you would like to have your song played, and we will make a plan.

What does the recording include? ▼

The recording process varies in type and length, and you choose the level of complexity of production. I can make you a simple recording fairly quickly and inexpensively, but you may also choose to add extra instrumentation and production. You may choose the number and types of instruments, the type of vocalist, and the all-around level of production.

What is the cost? ▼

Cost varies, depending on a number of factors including the number of people involved in the interviews, the level of production you choose for the recording, and if you choose to add a live performance. Please get in touch for a quote today!
